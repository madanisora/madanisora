## Research approach

- Prefer small experiments with a clear question and controlled scope.
- Record commands, tool versions, environment, inputs, outputs, and hashes when relevant.
- Distinguish observed facts from hypotheses and unverified conclusions.
- Keep challenge practice separate from competition results and independent research.
- Test only systems and targets for which I have authorization.

## Current direction

Developing deeper systems knowledge through hands-on C/C++, Linux, debugging, reverse engineering, memory-safety analysis, fuzzing, and root-cause investigation. These are active learning and project areas, not claims of mastery or professional certification.
